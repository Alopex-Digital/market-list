# Personal data note (EU / GDPR)

The list holds business contact details only: company emails and phones, names and roles of
people in their professional capacity, and public registry data.

Tell the user with the final file, in these words or close:

> The list contains business contacts of real people. Use them only for relevant B2B outreach,
> tell people where you got their details and let them opt out, and delete contacts you no
> longer need. Some countries have extra rules for email to named people, so check before a
> large campaign.

The skill never sends emails or messages itself.
